About

Who I Am

I'm Gauranga Gautam — known online as 0x9r4ngu. I work in offensive security: web application and API penetration testing, Active Directory attacks, and bug bounty hunting. I care about understanding why a vulnerability exists, not just popping it, and I document everything I find.

Most of my published work lives on my writeups blog — real assessment reports and step-by-step exploitation. I also build small security tools and the occasional awareness demo.

Outside security, I have a creative side: video editing, motion graphics, and design. Sometimes the two worlds collide.
