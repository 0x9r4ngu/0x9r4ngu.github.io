Who I Am

I'm Gauranga Gautam — known online as 0x9r4ngu. I work in offensive security: web application and API penetration testing, Active Directory attacks, cloud red teaming, and bug bounty hunting. I care about understanding why a vulnerability exists, not just popping it — and I document everything.

Most of my published work lives on my writeups blog — real assessment reports and step-by-step exploitation. I also build small security tools like the CVSS v4 calculator and awareness demos.
